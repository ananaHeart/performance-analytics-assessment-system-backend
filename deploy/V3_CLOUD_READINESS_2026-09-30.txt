CALENDAR BEHAVIOR
New academic years require Term 1, Term 2 and Term 3. Existing complete four-
quarter years remain supported, including their stored IDs and names. Editing a
planned legacy year retains its four periods; this does not convert old records.
One active term per academic year, non-overlapping dates, ownership checks,
manual overrides and completed-period restrictions remain enforced.

New term windows use inclusive starts and exclusive ends. Assessment openings
must precede the exclusive end; a closing time may equal it. Existing quarter
boundaries retain their previous behavior. The calendar UI supports both layouts
and renders the new terms in Manila time. Its current controls remain view and
refresh; this work does not introduce calendar editing forms.
