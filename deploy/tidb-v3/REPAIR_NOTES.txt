TIDB EXPRESSION COMPATIBILITY
The QR hash checks in V3_009 use BINARY qr_payload_hash REGEXP. TiDB prohibits
binary-string arguments to its regular-expression functions. The two cloud
expressions use REGEXP_LIKE(qr_payload_hash, '^[0-9a-f]{64}$', 'c') while keeping
qr_payload_hash = SHA2(qr_payload, 256). This preserves explicit lowercase
64-hex matching and the exact payload digest. Neither check is omitted or
weakened. Source and replacement expressions are both recorded in the JSON.

All other 122 named expressions are retained from the final migration state,
apart from whitespace normalization. Every referenced column was checked
against the exported schema. The offline validation does not prove TiDB DDL
acceptance, enforcement behavior, or correctness of live data. The two QR
variants especially require actual TiDB validation before release.

The term_order database constraint retains its source range 1..4. That supports
legacy four-quarter records as well as three-term records; application rules
must validate the number of periods per academic year. No term or date is
rewritten by this repair.
